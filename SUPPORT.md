# Getting help

* **How do I…?** Read the in-app **Guide** page and the [tutorials](docs/tutorials).
* **Questions and ideas:** open a thread in [Discussions](../../discussions).
* **Found a bug?** Open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) with the steps to reproduce it.
* **Security problem?** Do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

quantforge is maintained in spare time. Answers can take a few days.
