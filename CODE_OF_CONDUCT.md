# Code of conduct

quantforge adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussions about ideas and code — never about people. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainer privately (see the *Creator* page or the repository's
security contact). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and
restrict participation of anyone who breaks these rules.
